<h2>Rares,</h2>
<p class="soft">I made a place for you with my pauses. A place where words can breath before they shape themselves into sentences I cannot say always loud. It was created for the kind of love that does not shout, only a feeling that lingers just like when you hold my hand in the dark.
The letters here aren't messages, they are moments I've captured and decided to keep them in silence. In the silence when the world is asleep, when honesty feels safer than silence. Each word stays, each thought is held, nothing is hurried, nothing is lost.</p>

<p class="soft">Each day this week, a new page will sneak its way onto this little corner of the internet. Open it, follow the gentle hint it leaves just for you, and all that waits behind the door is yours. And if you catch yourself smiling… that’s part of it too.</p>
